## Enemy clipping correction

The original shared character sheet placed some predator tails and paws across uniform cell boundaries. Its predator cells are no longer used. Five individual transparent `enemy-*.png` images now supply map enemies, raids and inspector icons. The crop preserves alpha above 3 and five source pixels of padding; species-specific width budgets keep the complete silhouette visible. Den power badges sit above the animal area. `tests/e2e/frontier-enemies.mjs` validates transparent borders and all five den/inspector renderings. Original chinchilla art is unchanged.
