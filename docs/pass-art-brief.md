# Kumori Pass · art brief

Paintings for Kumori Pass (`/pass`), a night-time mountain-pass drift racer. Dora and Enzo's family runs the Hillside Bun Bakery at the foot of Mount Kumori, and every night before dawn Dora drives the old family hatchback, the Biscuit, up the pass with warm buns for the summit inn while Enzo reads the corners from his notebook. The street crews of the mountain challenge them to duels. The 3D road, cars and HUD are drawn by the game; these paintings are the story scenes, the speech-bubble portraits and the overtake cut-ins. The game shows its own drawn stand-in for any painting until the file loads. Written 02-11-2026.

**A new art style for this game: 1990s TV anime cel.** Bold clean black ink lines, flat two-tone cel shading with crisp shadow shapes, airbrushed night skies, dramatic speed lines, and painted backgrounds with neon-lit vending machines and mountain mist. No text, letters, numbers or logos in any image: the game prints its own. Everything is original: no real car makes or brand logos, and nothing from any existing anime, manga or game.

**Dora and Enzo (critical).** Adorable chinchillas, never mice: a plump round body, a big round head with a broad, slightly flat, smooth rounded crown with a soft short-fur edge, a short blunt muzzle, large round clean pink ears on the sides of the crown with nothing else on top of the head, and short whiskers. **Dora** has cream-white fur and ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** has cool blue-grey fur (never taupe, beige or brown), a pale grey chest and solid black eyes. They may wear small driving gloves; Enzo carries a pencil and a little notebook. Compare `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp` for the head and body shape; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits.

**The Biscuit.** A small boxy late-80s-style hatchback, cream with a sky-blue lower half and stripe, round headlights, and a round bun emblem on the door. Right-hand drive.

**Facing.** Dora drives from the RIGHT seat and Enzo rides on the LEFT, so Dora's cut-ins face LEFT (toward Enzo) and Enzo's face RIGHT (toward her). In the finale Enzo drives from the right seat, so `enzo-drive` faces LEFT. In the overtake cut-in the rival's panel sits on the left and Dora's on the right, so every rival portrait and cut-in faces RIGHT, toward the player's panel.

All files live in `public/art/pass/` as `.webp`. Wide images are 1536 × 1024 (shown cropped to wide panels with the face kept in the middle), portraits are 1024 × 1024.
