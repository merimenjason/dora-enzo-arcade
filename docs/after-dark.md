# The Arcade After Dark

Game 101, at `/after-dark`. Chapter One of a cabinet-hopping adventure. After closing time the Static (a crackling
violet glitch) is eating Dora and Enzo's arcade. The two chinchillas (Dora cream-white with red eyes, Enzo grey) hop
into the cabinets one by one: a candlelit castle, a sunken sea and a cloud fortress. Inside them they earn three
abilities that work in every world: Enzo's **ping**, Dora's **sweep** and the shared **gust**. They then follow the
Static to its anchor in the Deep and face the Static giant in the hub. It takes about 3 to 4 minutes, or longer if you
look for all 22 pearls.

Written 13-11-2026. The game began as a standalone prototype (`~/arcade-protos/arcade`, plain JS modules over a
canvas). This is the port into the arcade, with the same rules, tuning, art and feel, plus two fixes for the
prototype's weak spots ([What changed in the port](#what-changed-in-the-port)).

## Code layout

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `lib/afterdark-worlds.ts` | The hand-built worlds (the hub, the castle, the secret room, the sea, the Deep, the cloud fortress) and their types and constants (screen size, hit boxes, ping and gust reach). `makeWorlds` lays them out. The only randomness is the decorative phase of a few props, taken from the run's seed. |
| `lib/afterdark-core.ts` | The game state, `newGame` (with the `cloud`, `anchor` and `boss` presets the tests use), seeded randomness, events, sparks and particles. No DOM: sounds leave as `events` and music as `tune`. |
| `lib/afterdark-move.ts` | Walking, jumping, gliding, swimming, currents, the partner following the leader's trail, hurts, falls, hearts and checkpoints, and the three abilities with assist's smart choice. |
| `lib/afterdark-things.ts` | Each world's moving parts (armours, chandeliers, jellies, currents, storm banks, the pinwheel bridge, the anchor and its guards) and what a ping, a sweep or a gust does to them. |
| `lib/afterdark-flow.ts` | The chapter's order (`goal`), interacting, portals, the dive into a cabinet's screen and the exit, and the cutscenes. |
| `lib/afterdark-boss.ts` | The Static giant: its shroud, core and scatter phases, its slams and waves, three hearts. |
| `lib/afterdark-game.ts` | `step` (fixed 60 Hz), the wordless idle hints, which painted frame each hero shows, `warp` for the tests, and the save helpers. |
| `lib/afterdark-art.ts` | Generated by `scripts/afterdark-art.mjs` from the prototype packer's `art.js`: every frame's place in the 29 atlases and its anchor, plus the seven full paintings. |
| `lib/afterdark-paint.ts` | Drawing the worlds, Canvas 2D: parallax paintings, props, heroes, the Static, light pools, rings, gusts, particles, the dark of the grotto and the Deep, the giant, and the still of a cabinet's screen for the dive. |
| `lib/afterdark-scene.ts` | The layout, the camera, the HUD, the idle key prompts, the touch controls, the end card and the dive's zoom. Reads the engine and never changes it. |
| `app/after-dark/page.tsx` | The title (Play, or Continue from the hub), pause and settings. Keys, touch and gamepads, the loop, the save. |
| `app/after-dark/sound.ts` | WebAudio: eight small tunes and every effect, all synthesised. |
| `public/art/after-dark/` | 29 WebP atlases and 7 paintings (5 backdrops, 2 floor strips), 6.0 MB. |

`window.__afterdark()` returns `{ game, scene, screen, paused, save, scheme, start(from, seed?), warp(world, x?, y?),
sound }` for the browser tests. `main[data-ready="true"]` is set once the art has loaded.

## Controls

| | Keys | Touch | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | ← → or A D | the floating stick (left half) | left stick or d-pad |
| Jump, glide, swim up | Space, ↑ or W | JUMP | A |
| Use (a cabinet, a door, a shrine) | E | ACT | B |
| Ability (Enzo's ping, Dora's sweep) | J | ACT (when nothing is near to use) | X |
| Gust (once earned) | K | GUST (appears once earned) | Y |
| Swap who leads | Shift | SWAP | LB or RB |
| Pause | Esc or P, the II pill | the II pill | Start |
| Sound | M, the ♪ pill | the ♪ pill | |

Movement is direct: press and the hero walks, let go and the hero stops at once. Jump is higher the longer it is held.
In the clouds, holding Jump in the air glides. Underwater you swim in eight directions, Jump swims up, and letting go
leaves you hanging with only a gentle sink. Currents carry you, but you can still swim across or against them.

On a phone held upright, the play view sits at the top and the stick and buttons sit in a panel under it. On a phone on
its side, they sit in gutters to the left and right. The idle hint shows the player's own keys (arrows, WASD or the pad
labels), or a pulse on the touch button to press.

## The rules

**The chapter, in order.**

1. **Hub.** The castle and sea cabinets are lit; the cloud cabinet is dark behind a wall of Static.
2. **Castle without the ping.** The first hop is usually into the castle. Without the ping, the castle's cracked wall
   only knocks. A hop portal then opens straight to the sea.
3. **Sea.** Swim down to the conch and it gives Enzo the **ping**. The ping opens the pillar's tunnel into the dark
   grotto, lights crystals and finds the hidden sand mound.
4. **Castle with the ping.** Back in the castle, the ping reveals the hidden door. Behind it, in the secret room, the
   crest gives Dora the **sweep**. Sweep both braziers alight and the Static off the way out, and the portcullis lifts.
5. **Hub again.** Sweeping the hub's Static wall wakes the cloud cabinet.
6. **Cloud fortress.** An updraft lifts you, a puff drifts and a headwind pushes back. Halfway through, the shrine
   gives the **gust**. The gust blows storm banks away and spins the pinwheel into a bridge.
7. **The Deep.** Through the sea, the ping finds the anchor's heart, the gust strips its three orbiting guards, and the
   sweep dissolves its walls. The exit opens.
8. **The giant, in the hub.** Its fight is ping, gust, sweep, three times: the ping finds it in its shroud, the gust
   blows its core open, and the sweep scatters it. Then the arcade heals and every cabinet lights up.

**Assist** is on by default:

- Hurts cost nothing.
- J (or ACT) picks the ability the moment needs, and swaps to the hero who has it.
- The cloud fortress glides by itself over open sky.
- The chandeliers in the castle's pit catch a near miss with a wide margin (34).

**Assist off:**

- Three hearts. Losing all three starts you again at the last checkpoint passed in that world, or at the cabinet's door.
  The checkpoints are past the chandelier pit, and five through the clouds.
- J uses the leader's own ability (the wrong hero only reaches).
- You hold Jump to glide.
- The chandelier catch margin is 16.

**Pearls.** There are 22: 9 in the sea, 4 in the castle plus its chest, 7 in the clouds and 1 in the Deep. They are
optional, and the end card counts them.

**Hints.** The hints are wordless. After the player idles (5 s with assist, 8 s without), a keycap or touch pulse
points at the next step.

## What changed in the port

- **The chandelier pit with assist off.** In the prototype, a jump that missed a chandelier by a hair fell (the catch
  margin was 0 with assist off), and losing three hearts sent you back to the castle door, behind the pit again. The
  human-delay bots with assist off cleared 0 of 5 runs in 20 minutes, stuck in the castle with about 345 hurts and 175
  falls. Now the margin is 16 with assist off (34 with it on, as before), and with assist off there are checkpoints:
  one past the pit (x 3150), one before it (x 1990), and five through the clouds. Both bots now clear every run with
  assist off, and the castle takes them 43 to 67 s on average, down from about 490 s in the prototype with its own
  fixes.
- **The pearl no bot collected.** This was the castle chest behind a cobweb. Only Dora's sweep cleared the web, and the
  route never brings you back to the castle after the sweep is earned. Now Enzo's ping also shakes the web loose, on
  the pass through the castle that opens the hidden door. Assist offers the ping when you are near the web.
- **Ported, not rewritten.** The `this`-heavy prototype modules became pure functions over a typed `Game`. Its
  particles moved into the engine, so a seed replays a run exactly. Its sounds became events. The dive's screen still
  is made by the scene.

## The save

`after-dark-v1` in `localStorage`, read and written inside try/catch, so a private window plays on unsaved. It holds
assist, sound, music, the best run (the fastest time and the most pearls), the number of runs, and the chapter so far.
The chapter is saved whenever the heroes stand in the hub between cabinets: the abilities, the flags, and which
pearls were found by id (such as `sea:3` or `castle:chest`). Continue rebuilds the worlds from that. Finishing the
chapter clears it.

## The art

All of the art is painted, none of it drawn in code: the same Codex paintings as the prototype, copied unchanged.

- **The heroes.** Dora and Enzo have an outfit per world (pyjamas in the hub, tabards in the castle, swim gear in the
  sea, aviator coats in the clouds). Each outfit has a full set of animations: idle, walk, jump up and down, land,
  glide, swim and drift in a current, ping, sweep, gust wind-up and release, reach, hurt, cheer, the dive, a roll,
  brace, awe and riding.
- **The rest.** Props, the Static in its forms, the giant's eleven poses and its core, the HUD icons, the portraits, and the touch
  stick and buttons.

The atlases were packed by the prototype's tools. Copies of the Codex job list and scripts are in
`docs/after-dark-art/` (`jobs.txt`, `art.py`, `gen.sh`, `run-art.sh`, `sheet.py`); they use absolute paths under
`~/arcade-protos`. `node scripts/afterdark-art.mjs <path to art.js>` regenerates `lib/afterdark-art.ts` after a
repack. Nothing was missing, so nothing new was painted for the port.

## Sound

All of the sound is synthesised with WebAudio and stays silent until the first input.

- **Tunes.** Eight small tunes: the hub's night waltz, the sea's slow chords with bubbles, the castle's square-wave
  march, the cloud fortress's sparkles, the Deep, the secret room, the giant's drum-driven theme, and the healed hub in
  a brighter key.
- **Effects.** About twenty: the ping's rising chirps, the sweep's whoosh and chime, the gust, the Static's crackle
  (louder the nearer it is) and dissolve, knocks, slams and roars.

## Tests

- `npm run test:afterdark` compiles the engine into `.checks/` and runs `tests/afterdark.mjs`. It has 22 tests:
  - the save and resuming from it;
  - 22 pearls, and every frame and file the game asks for being shipped;
  - a seed replaying exactly;
  - direct movement, jumping, swimming and currents, and the partner following;
  - each beat of the chapter in order, from the wall refusing you to the giant's three hearts and the healing;
  - assist's smart ability, auto-glide and wide catch;
  - assist off's hearts and checkpoint;
  - the chest's web giving way to the ping;
  - the idle hints.
- `npm run bot:afterdark` (`-- --quick` for 2 runs each, `-- --verbose` for each run) plays the chapter with
  human-delay bots. They react 0.2 to 0.3 s late, misjudge jumps, and only know what a first-time player would see
  on screen:
  - **casual** follows the hints;
  - **curious** also looks for every pearl;
  - **random** mashes.
- `npm run test:e2e:afterdark` (with `npm run dev` running, or `BASE_URL`, and Playwright installed) presses every
  action with real keys and real touches at 1280×800, 390×844 and 844×390:
  - the title toggles and Play;
  - walking with the arrows and with WASD;
  - Space and Up jumping, and Shift;
  - E into a cabinet and the dive;
  - J's ping, K's gust, M, Esc and P;
  - the pause card's Resume, Title and Start over, and Continue;
  - the end card;
  - on touch: the stick both ways, JUMP, SWAP, ACT into a cabinet and ACT as the smart ability, and GUST once it
    appears.

  It checks for page errors and sideways scroll. `SHOTS=<dir>` saves screenshots. 124 checks.

| curious | on | 5/5 | 214 s | 37 s | 15.6 | 14.2 | 22.0/22 |

The bot script asserts these:

- casual with assist on clears every run, averaging under 260 s (the prototype's was about 202 s);
- curious clears every run, averages at least 21 pearls, and finds all 22 at least once;
- random clears at least 60% within 30 minutes;
- casual with assist off clears every run, with the castle averaging under 300 s.

Before the fixes, both bots with assist off cleared 0 of 5 runs.

## What is weakest

- **It is one chapter.** About 3 to 4 minutes for a casual player. The hub's harbour cabinet stays dark: it is the
  hook for Chapter Two.
- **The random bot gets lost in the cloud fortress.** It finishes only 2 of 3 runs within 30 minutes, stuck around the
  storm banks (x 3500 to 4650) and the castle's upper ledges. A mashing child could stall there too, though the hints
  keep pointing the way.
- **A keyboard on a phone held upright leaves a blank panel.** The panel under the play view is for the touch
  controls; when a phone is driven by a keyboard, the panel sits empty.
- **The end card draws over the hub.** The heroes standing in the hub still show behind the cheering pair.
- **The art is heavy for a short game.** 6.0 MB, all loaded up front behind a "✦ ✦ ✦" loading screen.
