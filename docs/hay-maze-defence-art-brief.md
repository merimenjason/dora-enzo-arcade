# Hay Maze Defence · art brief

Paintings for Hay Maze Defence (`/hay-maze`). They replace the small drawn icons on the menu (the crew and predator lists), in the tower palette, in the wave preview and on reward cards, plus a title painting on the menu. The board keeps its own animated drawings, where everyone is only about 16 px tall. Until a file loads, the game draws its own icon, so they can arrive in any order. Written 28-10-2026.

**Style for all images:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine brown ink edges, night-time mountain meadow lit by warm lantern light, matching `public/art/hayloft/dora.webp` and `public/art/hayloft/enzo.webp`. No text, letters, numbers or logos. **Dora** is a cream-white chinchilla with pink ears and ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a cool blue-grey chinchilla with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and solid black eyes (never taupe or brown). Predators are friendly-scary storybook villains, never gory. Export as `.webp` (quality about 88). All files live in `public/art/hay-maze/`.
