# Seedstorm · art brief

Paintings for Seedstorm (`/seedstorm`), a cosy vertical shoot-'em-up. Dora and Enzo fly dandelion-seed gliders up through five skies to calm the Grumble Storm, puffing mischievous storm critters into harmless dust. Nothing is ever hurt: beaten critters puff into fluff and drift off home. The game draws its own stand-in for every image until the file loads, so they can arrive in any order. Written 29-10-2026.

**Style for all images:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine brown ink edges, bright airy skies, matching how Dora and Enzo look in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`. **Their heads:** a broad, slightly flat crown with a soft edge of short dense fur, no spiky tufts or cowlicks on the head, and clean pink ear insides with no fluffy ear tufts (the older Hayloft Tycoon portraits have these tufts, so don't copy them). No text, letters, numbers or logos. **Dora** is a cream-white chinchilla with pink ears and ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a cool blue-grey chinchilla with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and solid black eyes (never taupe or brown). Villains are cute-grumpy, never scary or gory. Export as `.webp` (quality about 88). All files live in `public/art/seedstorm/`.
