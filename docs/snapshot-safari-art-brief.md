# Snapshot Safari art brief

Date: 27-10-2026

Every image Snapshot Safari loads, all under `public/art/safari/`, all WebP. Until an image loads (or if it never does) the game draws its own Canvas version, so these can arrive one at a time. 31 images in all.

**Style for all of them:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine brown ink edges, the same look as the other painted arcade games (see `public/art/roll/title.webp` and `public/art/disco/`). Creatures are inspired by real Andean wildlife, true to their colours and shapes, but friendly and rounded, never scary. **Dora** is cream-white with pink ears you can almost see through, ruby eyes and a white plume of a tail; **Enzo** is grey with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and face, and black eyes. **No text, letters, logos or borders in any image.**

Drawn behind the trail and tiled side by side as the camera scrolls, so **the left and right edges must join seamlessly**. Transparent sky: only the distant land is painted, filling roughly the lower 60% of the image, so the game's own sky (morning, noon or dusk, with its sun and clouds) shows above. The bottom 6% sits behind the ground line and can be plain. Painted in neutral daylight: the game tints it for dawn and dusk. No animals or people.

## Creatures (24)

One horizontal sheet per creature, **one 256×256 frame per behaviour, left to right in the order listed**, transparent background. In every frame the creature faces **right**, is horizontally centred, and stands (or sits, swims or hovers) with its feet or lowest point on the line **8 px above the bottom edge**; it is scaled so its larger dimension (the pose's width or height) spans about **240 px**. Flying poses: the bird's lowest point still near the bottom line, wings spread wide. The game gently bobs, squashes and tilts the frames, so keep poses clear and readable at small sizes. Its head is turned a little toward the viewer (three-quarter view) unless a pose says otherwise. No ground, rocks or branches: the game draws those (the bear's short trunk piece is the one exception).

## Painted (27-10-2026)

All 31 assets were painted with the built-in image generation tool. No image-generation CLI, API fallback or API key was used. Images are saved under `public/art/safari/`.

### References and finishing

Initial prompts used these supplied reference images, in this exact order:

1. `public/art/disco/dancer-dora-idle.webp` — Dora identity.
2. `public/art/disco/dancer-enzo-idle.webp` — Enzo identity.
3. `public/art/disco/venue-burrow.webp` — painting style.

Wildlife regenerations used only `venue-burrow.webp` as their painting-style reference to avoid carrying the chinchillas' character features into real species. The title regeneration used all three references in the original order. All calls set `transparent_background: false`: transparent assets were deliberately generated on the uniform green or magenta chroma matte specified in each exact prompt below.

Pillow and NumPy removed the mattes using the excess key-channel contribution, unmixed the background from soft edges, and suppressed residual chroma spill. Whole silhouettes were separated before cropping so an overlapping source bounding box could not cut a wing, tail or foot. Each pose was Lanczos-fitted, centred in its own 256×256 frame, and placed with its lowest point at y=247 (8 pixels of clear space below). Creature maximum dimensions are 240 pixels. Walkers use a 220-pixel fit, with the crouched pose compact. Landscapes fill the lower 60%, retain transparent sky above, and use an overlapping cyclic seam blend; two adjacent repetitions were inspected. The opaque title was fitted with `ImageOps.fit` and Lanczos. Every final file is WebP quality 88, with alpha for transparent assets.

Every saved WebP was reopened and fully decoded, with exact dimensions and RGB/RGBA mode asserted. Transparent images were checked composited over `#35313b`; poses were also inspected at the renderer's scale for 60 pixels per world metre, including the tiny birds and mammals. Edge checks found no visible green or magenta fringe and no high-saturation key-colour outliers among visible translucent pixels. The validation table records the final saved files.
