# Tidepool · art brief

Paintings for Tidepool (`/tide`), a cosy rock-pool ecosystem game. Dora and Enzo look after a rock pool on the shore below their summer cottage through the tides and four seasons, and keep a field journal of everything that lives in it. The game draws the pool, every creature, the tide clock and its own clay stand-in for every painting until the file loads, so the paintings can arrive in any order. Written 01-11-2026.

**A new art style for this game: claymation.** Everything is modelled in plasticine and photographed like a stop-motion set: chunky hand-pressed clay shapes with visible fingerprints and modelling-tool marks, slightly glossy highlights, soft contact shadows, and a warm, tactile seaside palette (sand ochre, sea teal, coral, kelp green, warm cream, gull white, slate rock). Soft daylight, a shallow depth of field. No text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo in the paintings (critical).** They are plasticine figures that read instantly as chinchillas: a plump round body, a big round head with a broad, slightly flat, smooth crown and a short blunt muzzle, large round pink ears on the sides of the crown with nothing else sticking up from the head, a big bushy tail and short whiskers. **Dora** is small and cream-white with ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a little bigger and cool blue-grey (never taupe, beige or brown) with a pale grey chest and solid black eyes. Compare `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp` for the head shape; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits. No clothes.

All files live in `public/art/tide/` as `.webp` (quality about 86).

The game keys the green out with Pillow, cuts each sheet into `dora-peer`, `dora-write`, `dora-bucket`, `dora-cheer` (and the same for Enzo), and trims each to its figure.

Dora sits at the left edge of the pool facing right; Enzo sits at the right edge facing left, so both look into the pool.
