# Whisker Saga painted artwork · 13-10-2026

Mode: built-in imagegen. Approved concept: `public/art/saga/reference.png` (documentation only, never a playable background). The renderer reads live battle state and preserves the original code artwork as a fallback.

## Production assets

All files are in `public/art/saga/`. `heroes-0.png` through `heroes-2.png` use 3 columns × 2 rows and follow the eighteen-entry `HEROES` order. Dora uses `hero-dora.png` for her dark natural ruby eyes. `portraits-0.png` through `portraits-2.png` use the same order, with dedicated busts rather than cropped seated bodies. `enemies.png` is 3 × 2: Scuttler, Spitter, Sneak / Bruiser, Hexer, Captain. `bosses-0.png` is 2 × 2: Thornback, Mudmaw / Old Croak, Rattlewing. `boss-4.png` through `boss-7.png` are complete individual Grimtusk, Frostfang, Cinderclaw and Hollow King sprites. Separate files keep wings and spikes away from atlas neighbours; they supersede the original second boss sheet. `land-0.png` through `land-7.png` follow `CHAPTERS` order.

`lib/saga-crops.ts` stores measured complete connected silhouettes. The cropper removes neighbouring fragments, preserves aspect ratio, adds an alpha gutter and anchors feet to the scene baseline. Generated transparency is preserved; Python only measured alpha bounds, never edited images. Portraits include full ears and whiskers. Enemy sheets share one colour scheme across chapters; bosses and landscapes are chapter-specific.

## Rendering and validation

`lib/saga-paint.ts` loads and crops cells on demand, retries missing requested files and exposes read-only crop diagnostics. The scene scrolls reflected panoramic tiles during marches, avoiding empty edges; reduced motion freezes scrolling and sprite movement. Breathing uses a subtle silhouette change anchored at the feet. Marching uses a slower, staggered step rhythm and eases into and out of scrolling. Attacks have a cooldown-based preparation, a short follow-through and a longer recovery; ranged units shift less than melee units. Enemy positions interpolate without changing battle distances. Victory hops stop after a short celebration, and defeated figures fade without flattening. Dora, Enzo and the scuttler use fixed painted rig parts with continuous joint motion; see `docs/saga-rig-art.md` for their component layouts and exact prompts. The pose-sheet experiments are retired. Other characters retain these restrained transforms. Projectiles, slashes, healing, shields, freezes, defeats and celebrations remain driven by battle events. DOM skill cards show actual energy; boss bars show actual health and wind-up. Neither labels nor state are baked into artwork. Engine numbers, saves and battle rewards are unchanged.

## Progressive startup

The menu and game become playable independently of image downloads. The saved squad and current chapter are prioritized; other hero, boss and landscape sheets are requested when rendered. Portraits refresh through an artwork subscription as each image arrives. Cropping is lazy rather than prewarming all fifty figures. Failed sheets keep their code fallback and can be retried. The private browser QA hook exposes `art.loadAll()` so the all-assets crop tests still inspect the complete library.

A local cold-load check with 100 ms latency and a simulated 10 Mbps download connection measured Set out becoming available at 54.8 seconds before this change and 5.8 seconds afterwards. These are development-server measurements, not a production-speed claim. `tests/e2e/saga-loading.mjs` holds every artwork request while starting and playing a battle, verifies later chapter assets remain unrequested, then releases the images and checks the scene updates.
