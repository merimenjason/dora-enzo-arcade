# Pillow Fort · art brief

Paintings for Pillow Fort (`/fort`), a physics stacking game. On a stormy evening Dora and Enzo build the greatest pillow fort the burrow has ever seen before bedtime, stacking cushions, bolsters, books, blankets and teacups into a fort that is tall, roomy and cosy, and that stays standing. The game draws the fort pieces, the physics, the rain and the lamp light itself, and draws its own felt-coloured stand-in for every image until the file loads, so the images can arrive in any order. Written 31-10-2026.

**A new art style for this game: needle-felted wool plush, as if from a stop-motion film.** Every image looks like a photograph of a handmade miniature set: needle-felted wool characters with a fuzzy felted texture and soft rounded forms, tiny glass-bead eyes with a single bright highlight, a few visible stitches, and fabric props with stitched seams. Warm miniature-set lighting from a little lamp, shallow depth of field with a softly blurred background. No paint strokes, no cartoon outlines, no text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo in this style (critical):** both are small needle-felted wool chinchillas. **Dora** is made of cream-white wool with ruby-red glass-bead eyes and clean pink felt ears. **Enzo** is a little bigger, made of cool blue-grey wool (never taupe, beige or brown) with a slightly darker blue-grey back, a pale grey chest and black glass-bead eyes, with clean pink felt ears. Their heads have a broad, slightly flat, smooth rounded crown of felted wool; nothing sticks up from the head except the two round ears (compare the head shape in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits). Neither wears clothes.

All files live in `public/art/fort/` as `.webp` (quality about 88).
