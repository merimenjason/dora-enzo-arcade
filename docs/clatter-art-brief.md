# Clatterbox · art brief

Paintings for Clatterbox (`/clatter`), a chain-reaction contraption puzzle. Dora and Enzo's workshop is a big cardboard box full of odds and ends, and every puzzle is a little cardboard diorama: place a few parts, press Go, and watch the clattering chain reaction. The game draws the boards, every part, the puppets of Dora, Enzo and the neighbours, and its own cardboard stand-in for every painting until the file loads, so the paintings can arrive in any order. Written 01-11-2026.

**A new art style for this game: cardboard craft diorama.** Everything is made by hand from household bits and photographed on a warm tabletop: corrugated cardboard with visible flutes on the cut edges, strips of cream masking tape, bottle caps, string, brass paper fasteners (split pins) as joints, wooden dominoes, marbles, acorns, and felt-tip marker doodles and labels. Soft warm lamp light from the upper left with gentle drop shadows and a shallow depth of field, like a stop-motion set. No text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo in the paintings (critical).** They are cardboard cut-out puppets: flat layered cardboard pieces, joined with brass split pins at the shoulders, with marker-drawn faces and painted colour. They read instantly as chinchillas: a plump round body, a big round head with a broad, slightly flat, smooth crown and a short blunt muzzle, large round pink ears on the sides of the crown with nothing else sticking up from the head, a big bushy tail and short whiskers. **Dora** is small and cream-white with ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a little bigger and cool blue-grey (never taupe, beige or brown) with a pale grey chest and solid black eyes. Compare `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp` for the head shape; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits. No clothes.

All files live in `public/art/clatter/` as `.webp` (quality about 86).
