# Honeycomb Hollow · art brief

Paintings for Honeycomb Hollow (`/honey`), a hex match-3 puzzle adventure. The bee colony in the hollow oak has lost the queen's honey map, and Dora and Enzo help the bees refill the honeycomb chamber by chamber, turning trios of nectar drops until they line up and pop into honey. The game draws the comb, the drops, the honey and every effect itself, and draws its own glowing stand-in for every image until the file loads, so the images can arrive in any order. Written 31-10-2026.

**A new art style for this game: stained glass.** Every image looks like a sunlit stained-glass window set in honey-coloured oak: bold dark leaded outlines dividing every shape into panes, luminous translucent colour with light glowing through (amber, honey gold, leaf green, rose, a little sky blue), subtle hammered glass texture and a soft light bloom. No paint strokes, no 3D render, no photo look, no text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo in this style (critical):** both are small chinchillas made of glass panes. **Dora** is cream-white and pale pearl glass with ruby-red glass eyes and clean pink glass ears. **Enzo** is a little bigger, cool blue-grey glass (never taupe, beige or brown) with a slightly darker blue-grey back, a pale grey chest and solid black eyes, with clean pink glass ears. Their heads have a broad, slightly flat, smooth rounded crown with a soft short-fur edge; nothing is on top of the head except the two round ears (compare the head shape in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits). Neither wears clothes.

All files live in `public/art/honey/` as `.webp` (quality about 88).
