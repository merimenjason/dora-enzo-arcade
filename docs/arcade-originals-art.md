# Faultline Rescue, The Night Wire and The Second Show · art direction

Paint scenic layers and sprites separately. Each field uses a 432×270 native pixel canvas, crisp square pixels and reserved sight lines around the nine action locations. The canvas draws the painted scene and actors. The DOM draws game feedback, labels, accessible controls and the forecast. These are readable at desktop scale and fit in a single column at 390 px.

| Game | Background and ground | Mechanism | Costume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faultline Rescue | Sienna rock, sand plaster, slate blue distance | Amber stress, pale cable, mint safe-route forecast | Dora rust rescue vest; Enzo indigo work apron |
| The Night Wire | Wet navy and slate, apricot windows, lavender surf | Oxidized copper jacks, lilac signal, amber queued call | Dora red rain slicker; Enzo pale sage operator vest |
| The Second Show | Wine velvet, honey boards, teal fly loft | Pale gold gaze, cyan route, brass cue cards | Dora plum satin waistcoat; Enzo practical dark green waistcoat |

All six character sheets have twelve separate poses: idle, step left foot, step right foot, jump, land, climb, interact, hurt, hero ability preparation, hero ability use, recovery and victory. Dora has cream-white fur, Enzo grey fur. Both have broad ears and fluffy tails. Costumes carry the setting without signature accessories.

Paint the field at 16:10 with upper, middle and lower tiers of three open stations. Keep the silhouettes of characters, the station markers and movement hints above the detail. Paint the objects and heroes on flat magenta. The pixel pass keys, crops, palette-snaps without dithering and packs to a strip; scenic sheets are downscaled to the field dimensions. The approved gameplay mockups are visual references for palette and mood.
