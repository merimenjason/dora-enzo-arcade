# Kite Day: art brief

Written 01-11-2026.

Kite Day is painted like a modern picture book: **loose watercolour and gouache**. Skies are wet-on-wet washes that bleed softly into each other; edges bloom and feather; the paper grain shows through the thin washes. Characters, kites and the important things you play with are painted in **opaque gouache** on top, with crisp hand-painted highlights, so they read clearly against the soft washes. No outlines in ink; shapes are defined by colour, a darker pooled edge and a light dry-brush highlight.

Palette: summer light. Cerulean and cobalt skies, warm cream paper, sap and olive greens, buttercup yellow, coral, poppy red, lilac evening shadows. The festival ending turns peach, rose and gold.

## The chinchillas

- **Dora**: cream-white fur, ruby-red eyes.
- **Enzo**: cool blue-grey fur (never taupe or brown), a pale grey chest, black eyes.
- Both: a broad, slightly flat, smooth rounded crown with a soft short-fur edge; two big round clean pink ears are the only things on top of the head. Reference `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp` for shape and colour (not paint style).
- They look up toward the kite in every pose.

## Drawn in code

Kites are drawn in code so their shapes can bank, flex and loop: each sail is a soft-edged wash of its colour with a darker pooled edge, a paper-grain overlay, the stencil pattern painted through a mask, and a crisp gouache highlight along the spars. Tails and streamers are ribbons that follow the kite. The ground, trees, treehouses, ponds, buildings, rings, parcels, balloons, bubbles and the dragonfly are painted in code the same way (layered translucent washes with a darker edge, then a few opaque gouache highlights), so they sit exactly where the wind and the physics put them. The wind itself is painted: drifting dandelion seeds, bending grass, streaks of cloud and travelling darker bands of grass where a gust is coming.

## Interface

Painted paper panels (cream paper with a torn, feathered edge and a soft wash shadow), hand-lettered headings in **Patrick Hand** and body text in **Nunito** from Google Fonts, medals painted as gold, silver and bronze rosettes.
