# Lucky Pips · art brief

Paintings for Lucky Pips (`/pips`), a dice-placement roguelite. A mischievous fog has rolled over the valley's harvest fair and grumpy fog-sprites in fair costumes have taken over the stalls. Dora and Enzo travel stall to stall with a pouch of carved wooden dice and a satchel of handmade tools to set the fair right. The game draws the dice, the felt tray, the tool cards and their icons, the map and every number itself, and draws a stand-in for every image until the file loads, so the images can arrive in any order. Written 31-10-2026.

**Style for all images:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine ink edges, a soft colourful harvest-fair palette (pumpkin orange, apple red, straw gold, candy pink and lilac fog, warm lantern light), matching how Dora and Enzo look in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`. **Their heads (critical):** copy the heads in those two paintings: a broad, slightly flat crown with a soft edge of short dense fur, smooth and rounded, and clean pink ear insides with nothing standing up from the head. Never use the Hayloft Tycoon portraits as a reference. No text, letters, numbers or logos. **Dora** is a small cream-white chinchilla with pink ears and ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a bigger cool blue-grey chinchilla with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and solid black eyes (never taupe, beige or brown). Neither wears clothes. The fog-sprites are soft, round, grumpy-cute creatures of pale lilac-grey fog wearing fair costumes; never scary, never green. All files live in `public/art/pips/` as `.webp`.
