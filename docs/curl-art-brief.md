# Sweep & Slide · art brief

Paintings for Sweep & Slide (`/curl`), a cosy curling game. The frozen millpond hosts the winter Pebble Cup, and Dora and Enzo's two-chinchilla team slides polished river stones at the painted rings against the valley's woodland teams. The game draws the ice sheet, the rings, the stones, the brooms and the measuring tape itself, and draws its own print-style stand-in for every image until the file loads, so the images can arrive in any order. Written 31-10-2026.

**A new art style for this game: linocut / woodblock print.** Every image looks like a hand-pulled linocut or woodblock print, like a vintage winter-sports poster: bold carved outlines, flat shapes in a limited set of inks (deep navy, ice blue, cream, warm red, with a little mustard yellow), visible print texture and grain where the ink is thin, and slight misregistration between the colour layers. No gradients, no paint strokes, no 3D render, no photo look. No text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo in this style (critical):** both are chinchillas carved in bold linocut shapes. **Dora** is small, printed in cream-white with ruby-red eyes and clean pink ears. **Enzo** is a little bigger, printed in cool blue-grey (never taupe, beige or brown) with a slightly darker blue-grey back, a pale grey chest and solid black eyes, with clean pink ears. Their heads have a broad, slightly flat, smooth rounded crown with a soft short-fur edge, and nothing sticks up from the head except the two round ears (compare the head shape in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits). They may wear a knitted warm-red scarf; nothing on the head.

All files live in `public/art/curl/` as `.webp` (quality about 88).
