# Paperfold · art brief

Paintings for Paperfold (`/fold`), an origami folding puzzle. A gust has scattered the pages of the valley's paper-lantern festival book, and Dora and Enzo fold sheets of coloured paper along their creases to make the lanterns, boats, cranes and stars the festival needs. The game draws the paper, the folds, the targets and the festival pieces itself, and draws its own paper-collage stand-in for every image until the file loads, so the images can arrive in any order. Written 31-10-2026.

**A new art style for this game: cut-paper craft.** Every image looks like a page from a children's picture book made of paper collage: layered torn and cut coloured paper with visible paper grain and fibres, soft drop shadows between the layers, slightly deckled torn edges, and a few fine pencil-line details. Backgrounds are paper-collage dioramas with clear depth layers. No paint strokes, no 3D render, no photo look. No text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo in this style (critical):** both are chinchillas built from cut and torn paper. **Dora** is small, made of cream-white paper, with ruby-red eyes and clean pink ears. **Enzo** is a little bigger, made of cool blue-grey paper (never taupe, beige or brown) with a slightly darker blue-grey back, a pale grey chest and solid black eyes, with clean pink ears. Their heads have a broad, slightly flat, smooth rounded crown with a soft short-fur edge cut into the paper, and nothing sticks up from the head except the two round ears (compare the head shape in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits). Neither wears clothes.

All files live in `public/art/fold/` as `.webp` (quality about 88).
