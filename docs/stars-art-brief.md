# Starstitch · art brief

Paintings for Starstitch (`/stars`), a cosy constellation puzzle. On clear autumn nights Dora and Enzo lie on the hill above the burrow with a spool of silver thread and stitch the loose constellations back into the sky. The game draws the stars, the seams, the thread, the clouds and the hill's edge itself, and draws its own stand-in for every image until the file loads, so the images can arrive in any order. Written 31-10-2026.

**Style for all images:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine ink edges, a deep night palette (indigo, violet and midnight blue, with silver starlight and warm gold), matching how Dora and Enzo look in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`. **Their heads (critical):** copy the heads in those two paintings: a broad, slightly flat crown with a soft edge of short dense fur, smooth and rounded, and clean pink ear insides with nothing standing up from the head. Never use the Hayloft Tycoon portraits as a reference. No text, letters, numbers or logos. **Dora** is a small cream-white chinchilla with pink ears and ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a bigger cool blue-grey chinchilla with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and solid black eyes (never taupe, beige or brown). Neither wears clothes. All files live in `public/art/stars/` as `.webp` (quality about 86).

## Dora and Enzo on the hill (green screen, 1536 × 1024)

Three moods of the pair lying together on their tummies side by side, as if on the grass, chins up towards the sky. One painting per mood, both chinchillas in it, nothing else. Background: a perfectly flat, uniform pure green (#00FF00), no shadow, no grass, no props.

| File | Mood |

| `pair-sleepy.webp` | Sleepy after a long night: Enzo yawning, Dora's eyes half shut, leaning on each other. |

## In the game

The green is keyed out with Pillow (a soft edge and green despill), trimmed and fitted to 960 × 480 standing on the bottom edge (the pair lies low and wide). The figures are levelled so their background is true black and drawn with the "screen" blend. A missing mood borrows `point`; the drawn chinchillas stand in until any painting arrives; a missing figure is left out, and the golden thread alone shows the constellation; a missing backdrop falls back to a drawn sky and hill.

## What shipped

All twenty-one paintings were made with Codex's built-in image tool, one image per job: the title, the five night skies and the ending at 1536 × 1024; the eleven figures, saved at 768 × 768 with their blacks crushed to true black for the "screen" blend; and the three moods of the pair, keyed and fitted to 960 × 480. Dora is cream-white with ruby-red eyes and Enzo cool blue-grey with black eyes in every one, both with smooth rounded crowns. The figures that shipped are the Snail, the Acorn, the Hare, the Lantern, the Sleeping Fox, the Owl, the Stag, the Leaping Trout, the Heron, the Teapot and the Two Chinchillas. Written 31-10-2026.
