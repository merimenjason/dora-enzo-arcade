# Ropeclimb · art brief

Paintings for Ropeclimb: Dora & Enzo Tied Together (`/ropeclimb`), a side-on climbing game. Dora and Enzo, tied together by a short rope, climb the inside of a giant hay-stuffed barn tower from the cellar to the weathervane on the roof, one leap at a time. The game draws its own stand-in for every image until the file loads, so they can arrive in any order. The game draws the rope itself. Written 29-10-2026.

**Style for all images:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine brown ink edges, cosy barn colours (honey wood, golden hay, warm lamplight), matching how Dora and Enzo look in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`. **Their heads (critical):** copy the heads in those two paintings: a broad, slightly flat crown with a soft edge of short dense fur, NO spiky tufts, cowlicks or forelocks anywhere on the head, and clean pink ear insides with NO fluffy ear tufts. Never use the Hayloft Tycoon portraits as a reference (they have these tufts). No text, letters, numbers or logos. **Dora** is a cream-white chinchilla with pink ears and ruby-red eyes and wears a small red cloth climbing belt round her middle. **Enzo** is a cool blue-grey chinchilla with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and solid black eyes (never taupe, beige or brown) and wears a small blue cloth climbing belt. Export as `.webp` (quality about 88). All files live in `public/art/ropeclimb/`.

## Dora and Enzo (transparent, 1024 × 1024, side-on, facing **right**, the whole body in frame, feet near the bottom centre)

Fourteen poses, seven each, all drawn at the same size so the game can swap them without a jump: Dora's files are `dora-<pose>.webp`, Enzo's `enzo-<pose>.webp`. No rope (the game draws it from the belt), no post or beam, no ground shadow.

| Pose | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `idle` | Standing on all fours, alert and cheerful, tail curled up behind. |
| `charge` | Crouched low and coiled like a spring, haunches bunched, eyes fixed up and ahead, ready to leap. |
| `leap` | Stretched out mid-leap upward and to the right, forepaws reaching high, hind legs kicked out straight behind, tail streaming. |
| `grip` | Clinging upright to an unseen post on its right: body vertical, all four paws' claws gripping forward, head turned to look up. |
| `swing` | Swinging through the air on the rope from the belt: body curved forward, legs tucked, ears blown back, thrilled grin. |
| `dangle` | Hanging from the belt with the rope above: body folded at the middle, legs dangling down, a little worried face looking up. |
| `cheer` | Sitting up on hind legs, both forepaws raised high in a happy cheer, eyes shut with joy. |
