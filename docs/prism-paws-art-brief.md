# Prism Paws · art brief

Paintings for Prism Paws (`/prism`), a light-beam puzzle game. On a night-time board, Dora and Enzo steer moonlight with mirrors, candy glass and crystals to relight the valley's festival lanterns without waking the sleeping kits. The game draws its own stand-in for every image until the file loads, so they can arrive in any order. Written 29-10-2026.

**Style for all images:** warm hand-painted toon storybook, soft cel shading, gentle fine brown ink edges, a cosy moonlit night with warm lantern light, matching how Dora and Enzo look in `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp`. **Their heads:** a broad, slightly flat crown with a soft edge of short dense fur, no spiky tufts or cowlicks on the head, and clean pink ear insides with no fluffy ear tufts (the older Hayloft Tycoon portraits have these tufts, so don't copy them). No text, letters, numbers or logos. **Dora** is a cream-white chinchilla with pink ears and ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is a cool blue-grey chinchilla with a darker wavy back, a pale chest and solid black eyes (never taupe or brown). Both have big round ears, long whiskers and fluffy tails. Export as `.webp` (quality about 88). All files live in `public/art/prism/`.
