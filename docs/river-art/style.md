# The River Remembers art direction

The watershed is an Andean landscape built from broad limestone terraces, reeds and limewashed embankments. Camera looks down at a three-quarter angle. Each gate, dock and interactable must have a distinct silhouette at phone size. Normal scenery stays quieter than those landmarks. No lettering in sheets.

| Area | Ground, walls and plants | Water, light and accents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reed Flats | flax limestone, reed green, earthen cream | turquoise, copper orange |
| Red Sluices | fired brick, burnt sienna, worn iron | blue-green, rust, ochre |
| Whitewater Steps | bleached chalk, spruce, grey slate | cold blue, white froth, ultramarine |
| Rain Cisterns | pale plaster, umber vault stone, lichen | violet navy, amber lamp, celadon |
| Blue Locks | cobalt glazed tile, sea-salt lime, dark brass | rich indigo, bright pale foam |
| Crown Spillway | apricot retaining stone, granite, dry sedge | broad teal, pale gold, soot |

Dora is cream-white and wears a persimmon wading coat, dark shorts and the short indigo river scarf. Enzo is grey and wears a quilted teal vest, canvas boots and the cork flotation belt. Hero inks stay dark violet-brown. Their feet align across frames; tails stay inside the cell. Paint a clear expression and a different pose for each action.

Paint separate sheets on solid flat green or magenta. Keep safe separation between cells. The import crops keyed objects and animation frames, applies a limited palette without dithering, and builds the game-size atlases. Terrain cells fill their footprint. In play, water is drawn from the same depth and current as the engine. Its foam, wake, tell, reflections and wind are painted overlays. The UI uses text and painted icons. Keep background scene paintings to the title, horizon and outro.

Each district has a four-panel continuous material sheet for ground, water, retaining stone and damp bank. The scene samples those panels across adjacent world tiles, so the walkable surfaces do not expose a repeated tile border. Collision and flow still come from the reach drawing.
