# Wind-Up Rally · art brief

Paintings and cut-outs for Wind-Up Rally (`/rally`), a side-on badminton duel played with wind-up tin toys. On a toyshop shelf after closing time, Dora and Enzo are tinplate clockwork chinchillas with brass keys in their backs, and they take on the shelf's other tin toys in the Clockwork Cup across a little lithographed tin court with a fine wire net. The game draws the court, the net, the shuttlecock, the scoreboard, the crowd and its own tin stand-in for every painting until the file loads, so the art can arrive in any order. Written 01-11-2026.

**A new art style for this game: vintage lithographed tinplate toys.** Printed tin with slightly offset litho colour registration, a glossy metal sheen with soft specular highlights, folded-tab seams, little rivets, a touch of chipped paint and wear at the edges, and brass wind-up keys. Lit like a warm shop window at night: amber lamplight, deep teal and plum shadows, a toyshop shelf behind (tin boxes, a jack-in-the-box, a toy train). No text, letters, numbers or logos in any image: the game prints its own.

**Dora and Enzo (critical).** They are tin wind-up toys that read instantly as chinchillas: a plump round body, a big round head with a broad, slightly flat, smooth crown and a short blunt muzzle, large round pink ears on the sides of the crown with nothing else on top of the head, a big bushy tail, printed whiskers and a brass key in the back. Each holds a small tin racket. **Dora** is cream-white printed tin with ruby-red eyes. **Enzo** is cool blue-grey printed tin (never taupe, beige or brown) with a pale grey chest and solid black eyes. Compare `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp` for the head and body shape; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits.

**Facing.** The player's toy plays on the LEFT of the court, so every Dora and Enzo cut-out faces RIGHT, toward the net, with the key on its back on the left. The game mirrors them for the right side. Every opponent stands on the right, so every opponent cut-out faces LEFT, toward the player.

All files live in `public/art/rally/` as `.webp`. Cut-outs are painted on flat #00FF00 and keyed with Pillow (soft edge, green despill), cropped and set on a transparent frame with the feet at the bottom.
