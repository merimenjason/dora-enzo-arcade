# Hero trials · 13-10-2026

The 80-stage campaign and tower remain intact. Six optional Trials open after 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 campaign clears. Each has three composed waves. First clears award a scroll and guaranteed gear at tiers 2, 3, 3, 4, 5 and 6. Replays grant gold without repeat first-clear gear or scrolls. Trial stars persist separately.

Fennel joins after Briar Relay: Epic Meadow mage, chain damage of 240%, 180% and 120% attack to three enemies. Sable joins after Clear Skies: Epic Summit healer, removes living allies' stuns and heals the weakest health-percentage ally for 400% attack. Trial recruits arrive at the current squad’s average level, capped by campaign progression. Neither can be summoned before recruitment; repeats are eligible afterwards.

Ascendant is tier 6: forge three tier-5 spare items, or earn one from the Crown Trial's first clear. The mobile Forge shows a three-column grid per gear type.

Save version 2 retains the saga-v1 storage key and accepts version-one saves. Old bags gain an empty tier-six entry and a separate zeroed trials array. Heroes, currencies, campaign stars, chests and tower progress are preserved.

Special effects use continuous gathering, impact and dissipation: sword arcs, thorn arrows, single shots, dawn fans, clover healing, musical rally notes, pollen blooms, spice haze, ice glyphs, protective domes and chain relays. Stable painted character anatomy is retained; no pose swapping. Reduced motion omits moving effects and pause freezes them.

Production art uses built-in imagegen. heroes-3.png contains Fennel/Sable bodies above their dedicated portraits. equipment-ascendant.png contains weapon/armour above charm/boots. Files are copied unchanged to public/art/saga; alpha measurements are read-only, runtime extraction preserves complete parts.
