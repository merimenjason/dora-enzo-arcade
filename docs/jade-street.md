# Jade Street

Game 105, `/jade-street`. A top-down open-city caper set on the night before the Lantern Festival in an invented harbour quarter. The Magpie Crew have stolen five lucky heirlooms, and Dora (cream-white fur, red eyes, jade bomber with a gold crane) or Enzo (grey fur, vermilion tracksuit top) wins them back over 25 story jobs. It is original: the quarter, its people, the jobs, the heat system and the interface were all made for this game.

## Code layout

The engine is plain TypeScript with no DOM, split one file per system. It steps at a fixed 1/60 s, takes all randomness from seeded generators, and emits an event queue (`fx`) that the page drains for the scene and the sound.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `lib/jade-rng.ts` | the seeded generator, string hashing and small maths helpers |
| `lib/jade-city.ts` | the 120 × 92 tile city (64 world units a tile): ground, solidity (open, building, water, tall, low), roof flags, the five districts, buildings, props, decals, places (markets, safe houses, garages, contacts, spots), 50 lucky cats, 13 murals, 21 hiding spots and 4 fire ladders; `passable()` per way of moving |
| `lib/jade-nav.ts` | flow fields per way of moving (`foot` may hop low tiles, `walk` may not, `small` and `big` rides, `boat`, `fly`), `downhill()`, `nearestFree()` and line of sight |
| `lib/jade-move.ts` | direct-control movement for capsule bodies with wall sliding, the five rides' numbers, and on-foot numbers |
| `lib/jade-traffic.ts` | lane-following cars and wandering pedestrians, bumps and honks |
| `lib/jade-heat.ts` | lanterns, wardens, roadblocks, the blimp, hiding, evading and getting caught |
| `lib/jade-trade.ts` | the six goods, each market's leanings, nightly drift, tips, spreads, haggling, capacity, buying, selling and the stash |
| `lib/jade-story.ts` | the people, the five heirlooms and the 25 jobs as data (steps: talk, goto, pickup, deliver, ride, chase, tail, escort, race, stealth, collect, mini, heat, trade, fares) |
| `lib/jade-mission.ts` | runs a job: each step's rules, the actors (thieves, Pip, the lion, lookouts, racers, fares), checkpoints and their restore |
| `lib/jade-gigs.ts` | the 13 side-gig boards, each building a short job |
| `lib/jade-mini.ts` | the four mini-games as engines: lock, wires, wok and dumplings |
| `lib/jade-save.ts` | the save (`jade-street-v2` in localStorage, v1 migrated), outfits, paints, upgrades, safe houses, trophies, the daily event |
| `lib/jade-game.ts` | `World`: the player, rides, the step order, interaction, the clock, magpies, trading through the world, safe-house actions, routes and the objective |
| `lib/jade-art.ts` | generated by `scripts/jade-art.py`: every frame's atlas rectangle, its painted share (`FILL`), the textures and plain images |
| `lib/jade-scene.ts` | Canvas 2D drawing: ground baked into 8 × 8 tile chunks (an LRU of 44), painted buildings with baked soft shadows, y-sorted sprites, hue-shifted outfit and paint variants, code-drawn glows, headlights, lantern strings, spotlights, fog over closed districts, the route line and the minimap |
| `app/jade-street/controller.ts` | the loop, keys, the touch stick, the fixed steps, saving every 5 s, music changes, and the `window.__jade` hook |
| `app/jade-street/page.tsx`, `hud.tsx`, `panels.tsx`, `minis.tsx`, `ui.tsx`, `sound.ts`, `jade.css` | the title, HUD, dialogue, failure card, touch controls, market, safe house, garage, map, pause, mini-game screens and WebAudio sound |

The React HUD re-renders about ten times a second, or at once when a panel, dialogue or failure opens; the world itself is never rendered by React.

## Rules and numbers

**Moving.** On foot: walk (sneak) 145, run 235, a dash at 560 for 0.22 s (0.75 s cooldown), and a hop of 0.42 s that clears low tiles (crates, hedges, benches). Controls are direct: the stick or keys set the heading and speed, with no drifting. Rides:

| Ride | Top speed | Hold | Moves on | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scooter | 340 | 8 | small (alleys too) | some are locked and need a jump-start |
| Trishaw | 250 | 16 | big | a seat for a fare |
| Tuk-tuk | 310 | 20 | big | a seat for a fare |
| Delivery van | 285 | 40 | big | too wide for alleys |
| Canal boat | 250 | 30 | water | the canal and harbour |

Boost (Space on a ride) and honk (Q) replace dash and hop. Four fire ladders lead to the rooftops; up there you cross gaps by hopping, nobody adds heat, and a hop that would land off a roof puts you back where you took off.

**Heat.** Up to five lanterns. Bumping a warden (dashing on foot, or riding over 130) adds 0.3, knocking a roadblock 0.25, and hitting a car above 160 adds 0.15; on Assist these count half while a job is running. Wardens on bicycles come one per lantern (one fewer on Easy, at least one), up to four, from behind or to the side of the way you are heading; they ride at 205 on Easy or 240 on Normal plus 12 a lantern. From three lanterns roadblocks of fruit crates appear on the road ahead of a ride; from four a blimp follows where you were last seen, and its spotlight sees you within 120. Wardens see 520 with clear line of sight. Out of sight for 3.2 s (Easy) or 4.6 s (Normal) puts a lantern out, twice as fast in a hiding spot. A jacket swap at a safe house clears all heat. A warden who reaches you on foot (0.35 s on Easy, 0.2 s on Normal) or beside a stopped ride (1.1 s) catches you: a fine of 5% of your coins (10 to 150), a trip to the nearest safe house, and any job restarts from its checkpoint.

**Trading.** Base prices: jade bangles 120, porcelain bowls 58, silk fans 44, mooncakes 34, tea 22, mangoes 12. Each market leans 0.68 to 1.38 on each good (the Docks sell jade cheap, the Arcade Strip pays most for it, Temple Hill sells tea cheap and the Canal Quarter pays for it, and so on), every night drifts each price by up to 14% either way, a contact's tip multiplies one good at one market, and the daily event adds one more. Buying costs 6% over the mid price and selling pays 6% under it, 3 points better for each reputation tier (20, 50 and 80). The haggle needle: in the gold middle the stall's prices improve by 14%, in the green by 7%, outside it nothing, and a sour miss costs 4%; the zone is wider with reputation and on Assist. You carry 4 on foot (plus one per cargo upgrade) or your ride's hold (plus a quarter of it per upgrade). Magpies swoop every 25 to 45 s while you carry anything worth having (a chance of up to 50% by cargo value, 60% of that on Assist) and take two of your most valuable good unless you dash, boost, hop or hide as they reach you.

**Progress.** Each job pays coins and reputation in its district. Safe houses cost 150 to 300 and 15 reputation; each has a stash (60, plus 20 per cargo upgrade), the jacket swap, sleep till the next night (new prices), and the choice of hero. Six outfits per hero (120 to 400 coins and 20 to 30 reputation in a district), three garage upgrades with three levels (engine +7% top speed a level for 150, 400, 900; tyres +10% steering for 120, 350, 800; racks for 100, 300, 700) and five paints (free to 120) that apply to every ride. A night lasts 720 game minutes, one a second (a little slower on Assist). There are 20 trophies.

**Assist** (on by default, in Settings): a gold route line to the objective and the nearest job, gentler timers and lookouts, slower and rarer magpies, half heat during jobs, a wider haggle zone, larger mini-game windows and a slower clock. **Difficulty** is Easy (default) or Normal and changes the wardens and the evade time.

## The city

Five districts, each with a market, a safe house and contacts: the **Night Market** (south-west; shophouses, stalls, the wok stall and the first garage), **Temple Hill** (north-west; the temple, its hundred steps and the pagoda), the **Canal Quarter** (the middle; a canal with bridges, jetties and boats; the second garage), the **Docks** (south-east; warehouses, a container yard, the pier) and the **Arcade Strip** (north-east; neon). The map screen shows closed districts under fog and labels them. A pin dropped on the map (when no job is running) draws a route to it. Parked rides are placed out of the traffic; boats moor along the canal and harbour edges.

Each chapter's last job is a chase for the heirloom (the third, The Pearl Lantern, is on the canal by boat; the last is three chases against Madame Glint). Every job has checkpoints; a failure restarts it from the last one, with your ride, what you carry and your heading as they were then.

## Art pipeline

Codex paints every sprite, building, ride, person, prop, ground texture, mini-game scene, portrait and interface piece; code draws only light, glow, shadow, fog, lantern cables and route lines. The prompts are in `docs/jade-street-art/jobs.py` with the shared style in `style.txt`, and `gen.py` runs Codex image generation (up to six at once). The painted mock-ups that set the look are `mock-play.jpg`, `mock-city.jpg` and `mock-trade.jpg` there. `python3 scripts/jade-art.py <folder of paintings>` keys out the flat background, removes the magenta cast from fur and jackets, keeps Dora's fur cream-white, cuts each sheet into frames, scales them to world size, packs six WebP atlases (5.6 MB with the textures and plain images, in `public/art/jade-street/`) and writes `lib/jade-art.ts`. Each hero has walk and run cycles in three facings, idle, sneak, dash, hop, carry, bump, pickup, hide, climb, talk, swap, paint, pick, door, sleep, wave, cheer, caught, and riding poses for the scooter (and a bump and a cheer on it), the trishaw's pedals and the boat's pole; in a tuk-tuk or van the hero is inside the cab. Outfits and paints are hue shifts of the painted frames, cached per variant.

## Tests

- `npm run test:jade`: 21 engine tests, listed in the README guide. The two slowest play every job with a scripted solver (`playMission` from `tests/jade-bot.mjs`, perfect profile), then play each non-dialogue step again with a failure forced the first time it is reached, to prove every checkpoint restores to a finishable state.
- `npm run bot:jade`: a casual bot (reacts 0.2 to 0.3 s late, wobbles its heading by up to 0.28 rad, misses mini-game timing by up to a whole window) plays every job over ten seeds on Assist and Easy and must finish each at least 90% of the time; a Normal bot (Assist off, Normal difficulty, slightly steadier) is reported. `--quick` (three seeds, one Normal run) is part of `npm test`.
- `npm run test:e2e:jade`: 394 checks of every player action at 1280×800 (keys and mouse), 390×844 and 844×390 (real multi-touch through the DevTools protocol, so the stick stays held while another finger taps Dash or Honk), with sideways-scroll and contrast checks on every screen and a failure on any page or console error. `SHOTS=<dir>` saves the screens.
- `npm run play:jade`: plays two minutes in each district at 1280×800 with keys, rides, five-lantern heat and constant jumps to fresh streets, and fails under 55 fps.

### Results (14-11-2026)

Bots, ten seeds per job: the casual bot finished all 25 jobs every time (100%), first time without a retry in 242 of 250 runs; its slowest first-try jobs were Container Maze (6/10) and Neon Night Race (7/10). The Normal, Assist-off bot finished all 25 in 4 of 4 seeds, but needed a retry in every seed on Container Maze and Neon Night Race and in two of four on Hot Goods. Average job length 18 to 87 s for the bot.

Frame rate on this Mac in headless Chromium at 1280×800, two minutes per district: Night Market 59.8 fps average (lowest half-second 56.0), Temple Hill 60.0 (56.0), Canal Quarter 60.0 (58.0), Docks 60.0 (58.0), Arcade Strip 59.9 (56.1). No page errors.

## What is weakest

- **It is easy.** The bots, even with Assist off on Normal, finish every job. A player who knows action games will find the wardens forgiving and the jobs short; the challenge is in the stealth and racing jobs and in trading well, not in surviving. Normal could be harder.
- **The city reads as a grid.** The streets are laid out on blocks with alleys between; it is legible and every road is reachable, but it is less organic than the mock-ups.
- **The painted buildings are seen from above at a slant**, and some neighbours repeat. The camera is higher and more top-down than the play mock-up, as asked, which shows more roof than shopfront.
- **Traffic is simple.** Cars follow lanes and stop for what is ahead; there are no traffic lights or turns signalled, and pedestrians wander rather than go anywhere.
- **Phones are untested on real hardware.** The touch suite runs in Chromium's phone emulation, and the frame-rate run is desktop only.
- **The contrast check skips text over paintings or the canvas**, where no single backing colour exists; those labels rely on their dark plates and outlines.
- **No pins during a job**: the map shows the job's route instead.
- **Sound is synthesized**: three WebAudio tunes (menu, street, chase) and effects, no recorded audio or voices.
