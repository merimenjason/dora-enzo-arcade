# Woodland Band · art brief

Paintings for Woodland Band (`/band`), a music-making toy and puzzle game. Dora and Enzo lead a band of woodland animals who play the clearings of the forest: a hedgehog on drums, a woodpecker on wood blocks, a frog on double bass, a mole on tuba, an owl on flute, a bird choir, Dora on ukulele and Enzo on glockenspiel. The game draws everything in play (the stage grid, the clearings, every musician, the audience, and Dora and Enzo) in code, and draws its own stand-in for each painting until the file loads, so the paintings are extras. Written 01-11-2026.

**A new art style for this game: mid-century cartoon (1950s limited animation).** Flat geometric shapes, a limited palette of mustard yellow, teal, coral, cream and warm ink black, with a deep teal-green night or forest ground. Colour fills sit slightly offset from their outlines, like a print where the plates don't quite line up. Ink lines are textured and dry-brushed, not smooth. Starburst and boomerang motifs, stylised lollipop and triangle trees, flat stage spotlights. No gradients beyond a gentle flat grain. No text, letters, numbers or logos.

**Dora and Enzo (critical).** Drawn as mid-century cartoon characters from simple geometric shapes, adorable and expressive. They must read instantly as chinchillas: a plump round body and a big round head with a broad, slightly flat, smooth crown; a short blunt muzzle; large round pink ears on the sides of the crown with nothing else sticking up from the head; a big bushy tail and short whiskers. **Dora** is cream-white with ruby-red eyes and holds a coral ukulele. **Enzo** is cool blue-grey (never taupe, beige or brown) with a paler grey chest and solid black eyes, with a glockenspiel and two mallets. Compare `public/art/driftwood/title.webp` and `public/art/roll/title.webp` for the head shape; never the Hayloft Tycoon portraits. No clothes.
