Art direction: polished hand-painted 2D game art with bold clean ink outlines and flat cel-shaded colour blocks, like a premium handheld top-down city game, viewed from a high top-down camera with a slight 3/4 tilt. Setting: an original, fictional night-time Chinatown-style harbour quarter of a Southeast Asian port city: rows of two-storey shophouses with five-foot-way arcades, painted shutters, red paper lanterns strung across streets, glowing shop signs that are pure colour shapes, night-market stalls with striped awnings, steaming woks, a temple gate with curved tiled roof, a canal with a small stone bridge, tiled rooftops seen from above. Palette: deep indigo and teal night shadows lit by warm lantern red, vermilion, jade green, gold and pink neon glows; wet streets reflecting the lights. Cheerful, cosy, cartoonish and adventurous, never scary, no weapons, no blood. An entirely original design that copies no existing game.
The heroes are two anthropomorphic CHINCHILLAS (not mice, not rats): short rounded muzzle, very big round pink-lined ears, plump round body, big fluffy tail, small paws. Copy ONLY their faces, eye colours and fur colours from the attached reference pair and IGNORE every accessory in it (no goggles, caps, headphones, scarves, jackets, bags). Dora has cream-white fur and red eyes and wears a jade-green satin bomber jacket with a gold embroidered crane on the back and black cropped trousers. Enzo has soft grey fur and dark eyes and wears a vermilion zip-up tracksuit top with white piping and baggy dark cargo shorts. No hats. They carry no signature items.
The rivals are the Magpie Crew: cheeky anthropomorphic magpies in black-and-white plumage with glossy blue-green wing sheen, wearing matching purple windbreakers, who snatch things; plus portly raccoon market wardens in navy uniforms with whistles as the comic "heat".
The UI is painted like enamel shop signs and lacquered wood: rounded jade and vermilion plaques with gold trim, glowing paper-lantern icons.
Absolutely no text anywhere: no letters, words, numbers, Chinese characters, logos or symbols, not even blurry fake writing. Where text or numbers would go, leave blank painted plaques. Signs are blank coloured shapes.
