landscape
A title-screen painting (no text at all) for the original tower-defence game: a wide sunny Andean valley at the start of the harvest festival, colourful bunting and woven awnings in a stone village plaza in the foreground, terraces climbing the slopes, snowy peaks behind. Over the mountain pass on the left a parade of cute grumpy colour-coded storm-puffs and one big dark Thunderhead is drifting down toward the valley. In the right foreground Dora and Enzo (the two chinchilla heroes in their festival outfits as in the attached mock-ups) stand on a low stone wall, Dora pointing at the clouds with a determined grin, Enzo with his paws on his hips. Leave the upper-left third of the sky calm and simple (a soft clear sky) so a title can be placed there by the game.
