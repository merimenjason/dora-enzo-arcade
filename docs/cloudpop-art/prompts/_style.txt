Art direction: polished hand-painted 2D game art in a bright gouache storybook style, soft painted edges with crisp readable silhouettes, warm Andean highland harvest-festival palette (terracotta, marigold, saffron, turquoise, rose red, sunlit greens) set against cool storm-blues and indigos for the weather. Sunny, cheerful, cartoonish and cosy, never scary. Soft top-left sunlight. An entirely original design that copies no existing game: no monkeys, no balloons, no darts.
The heroes are two anthropomorphic CHINCHILLAS (not mice, not rats): short rounded muzzle, very big round pink-lined ears, plump round body, big fluffy tail, small paws. Copy ONLY their faces, eye colours and fur colours from the attached reference pair and IGNORE every accessory in it (no goggles, caps, headphones, scarves, jackets, bags). Dora has cream-white fur and red eyes and wears a short coral-red embroidered Andean jacket with flower embroidery over a saffron wrap skirt. Enzo has soft grey fur and dark eyes and wears a turquoise-and-ochre striped poncho with tassels and a woven sash. No hats on either. They carry no signature items.
The enemies are round, puffy, cute storm clouds with small grumpy faces (storm-puffs), colour-coded by layer: pale white wisp, sky-blue drizzle, teal-green shower, golden-yellow squall, coral-orange gale. Bigger specials: grey hail puff studded with ice chunks, dark charcoal rumble puff crackling with tiny sparks, frosty pale-cyan frost puff with ice crystals, and a huge dark anvil-shaped Thunderhead boss cloud with lightning in its belly.
The game UI is painted in the style of an Ayacucho retablo: chunky carved wooden frames and plaques painted with bright folk-art flowers and zigzag borders in turquoise, rose and marigold, round wooden buttons, woven-cloth strips.
Absolutely no text anywhere: no letters, words, numbers, logos or symbols, not even blurry fake writing. Where numbers would go, leave blank painted plaques.
