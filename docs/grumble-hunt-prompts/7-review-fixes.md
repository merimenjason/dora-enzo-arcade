A review of the first Grumble Hunt build found these bugs, each confirmed in the code. Fix all of them, keeping the engine deterministic and the style of each file (hand-formatted; don't run oxfmt). For each one, add a test where an engine test can show it: in `tests/grumble-hunt.mjs` for the engine, or `tests/e2e/grumble-hunt.mjs` for page input.

1. **Space or Enter presses a clicked button again.** `app/hunt/page.tsx:88`: after a click on a pouch button (`:157`) or the sound button (`:136`), focus stays on the button. Space (roll) then uses another item or toggles sound instead of rolling. Return focus to the meadow (`focusWorld()`) at the end of the pouch, sound and other in-hunt HUD button handlers. Leave panel buttons alone.
2. **No lock-on while sprinting.** `page.tsx:90` ignores Shift+Tab, and Shift is sprint, so Tab while sprinting moves focus out of the page and pauses the hunt. When the meadow or body has focus in a hunt, treat Shift+Tab as Tab, calling `preventDefault`.
3. **Dizzy chains.** `lib/grumble-hunt-game.ts:163` adds heavy-hit build-up while the beast is already dizzy, so one heavy hit right after it recovers makes it dizzy again. Don't add build-up while it's dizzy.
4. **Charges and swoops overshoot their orange lane.** The lane is 12 units long (`:267`), but the charge moves at 12 and the swoop at 14 for 1.05 s (`:283`, `:288`), so they travel 12.6 and 14.7. Make the active time the lane length divided by the speed, or stop the beast at the lane's end, so no beast moves past what the marker shows. Add a test that a hero standing just beyond the end of the lane, and outside the hit radius of its end, is never bumped.
5. **Taps during hit-stop are lost.** `step()` returns early during hit-stop (`game.ts:315`), but the page clears the tap pulses after every step (`page.tsx:63`). A Space tap in the 0.08–0.11 s after a bonk never rolls. Keep the pulses until a step actually runs: clear them only when hit-stop was 0 before that step, or buffer taps in the engine.
6. **A charge released during cooldown is thrown away.** At `game.ts:224`, charge builds once the swing has ended, but `attack()` (`:197`) returns while `cooldown > 0`, and `:225` resets the charge anyway. Enzo holding K right after a heavy shot, then releasing, fires nothing. Build charge only when `cooldown <= 0`. The HUD charge display must match.
7. **Buddy Dora's hammer reaches Skreel in the air.** `game.ts:151` checks only the player's Dora. Block hammer bonks on a high Skreel for whichever chinchilla holds the hammer, leader or buddy, as `docs/grumble-hunt.md` says.
8. **Getting tuckered out leaves drops in the meadow.** `bumpHero` (`:182`) doesn't collect drops still on the ground, and they vanish. Collect them in the zero-Fluff branch, as calming a beast does (`collect(true)`), so a tuckered-out hunt keeps everything it shed.
9. **Wrong text about dizzy build-up.** The field guide (`page.tsx:187`) and `docs/grumble-hunt.md:25` say "two charged hammer bonks", but charged seed shots and buddy bonks add to it too. Change the text to match the code: "two big bonks or big shots; buddy bonks help a little".
10. **The unlock banner repeats.** "Hornclack and Frostmane are now on the hunt board!" (`page.tsx:177`) shows on every result screen after the unlock, tuckered-out ones included. Show it only on the result of the hunt that made the third different nap.
11. **Keyboard dead after a lost WebGL context in the village.** The context-lost handler (`page.tsx:44`) pauses in any mode, and keydown (`:91`) drops every key while paused. Pause only during a hunt, and unpause or recover on `webglcontextrestored`.
