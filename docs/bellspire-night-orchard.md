# The Night Orchard

The old caravan fed itself from orchards cut into the mountain above the lake. The Tollkeeper kept the gate shut when the grafts stopped flowering. Now the branches turn their leaves toward Enzo’s light, and Dora can cut the ties that have held them in place for years. Killa, the orchard keeper, tends the last warm lamp.

Use the orchard wicket in the Salt Tollhouse after calming the Last Tollkeeper. It opens onto the Graft Terraces. Beyond the terraces, the Root Cellars trade open sky for clay cisterns, ladder wells and warm lamps. Keep the walkable edges bright enough to read with the lantern lowered. The terraces use ochre stone, dark pine silhouettes and golden buds. The cellars use russet clay, warm white roots and blue-grey water.

## The two moves

**Enzo — Lantern Bloom.** Use at a golden bud to wake a dormant graft. Leaves spread and settle into a solid one-way ledge. The edge appears as soon as it can hold weight, and the platform stays for the rest of the save. The helping hand points to the bud, names Enzo, and shows the player's tag or use binding. The prompt disappears when the graft opens or the player leaves.

**Dora — Graft Shear.** Attack an ochre tie on a living graft to release its braced limb. A second ledge settles into reach and stays there. Dormant roots give no strike reaction and the helping hand points back to the bud. When the graft is ready, the prompt names Dora and shows the tag or attack binding. Existing footholds stay put during both actions. Every platform comes from a hand-specified span shared by the scene, physics and planner.

The progression is a single low branch over a safe basin, a bloom followed by a shear from the same landing, two independent grafts worked in succession from different heights, and a fork where one route combines the grafts with an old ability. Both heroes can traverse the completed routes. Falls in the teaching rooms lead back to the approach.

## Room route

**Orchard Wicket (0,17).** Arrive at a stone porch with a return wicket and the first candle. Killa stands at the edge of a shallow dip. Walk down to her, climb the orchard steps, then drop to the east path past Enzo’s pedestal. A ladder takes curious players to a ledge above the porch before they move on. This room teaches the look of a dormant bud without asking for a jump.

**Nursery Wall (2,17).** Walk into a wide nursery basin with steps back to both ends. Enzo wakes a single bud from solid ground. The low branch gives the shortest path to the eastern bank, while the floor and a ladder still give a way out. An upper perch with a chest rewards using the new branch. Players see the branch settle before jumping onto it and can practise repeatedly.

**Graft Terraces (4,17).** Pick up Dora’s Shear at the low landing. Bloom the lower graft, then cut the ochre tie while standing on the same shelf to let the upper branch down. Climb the two branches across a ravine with a floor below, then land by the candle. A mural on the high rim uses the upper spur. This is the first required tag, with a safe way back from the ravine.

**Bracken Stag (6,17).** Enter from a raised ramp, with a shallow central run and stepped shoulders. Enzo blooms an arena graft so the charging Stag catches its antlers. Dora cuts the marked tie beside its trapped head to expose the flank. The charge has a planted hoof tell, and the marked perch gives a safe place to wait. A ledge above the run gives a second way to change sides. Exit down the eastern ladder after the Stag is calm.

**Seed Steps (6,18).** Descend into the high east side, then walk west over uneven planting beds. A low spike in the first bed is visible before dropping in, with a high ledge as an alternative. A second bud high on the slope makes the way back up clearer. One orchard foe patrols a lower bed, leaving room to recover and line up the jump separately.

**Pruning Walk (4,18).** Enter high, work downhill through the first graft, then turn back uphill on a low shelf to reach a second bud. Use Dora’s shear on the second tree from that shelf to reach the west exit. A chest on the dead branch over the first bowl is an optional detour. The two grafts have their own silhouettes and lighting, so the next target is visible before tagging.

**Double Espalier (2,18).** A tall retaining wall splits the room. On the low route Enzo opens a graft, then Dora releases the tie above the first branch to reach the ridge. An upper route uses an existing Bell Rope hook as a shortcut and leads to a mural. Drop into the west bowl and climb to the cistern path. The fork lets players choose a familiar move or the new pair.

**Cistern Candle (0,18).** Rest by the lamp above a disused stone tank. Steps descend around the tank to a safe shelf, with a chest reached by climbing out the far side rather than retracing the steps. The forward route goes down the west ladder after the shrine. The room gives the player a chance to read the map and breathe before the tighter cellars.

**Silt Ladder (0,19).** Descend at the west end, past a side ledge and into a broad basin. A crystal and a graft span different parts of the basin. Ring the crystal for the high route or use the graft over a lower ledge to reach the same stone landing. A raised dry shelf at the east end offers solid ground before the next doorway. Here players choose between the familiar temporary bridge and permanent growth.

**Root Vault (2,19).** A crescent of clay steps surrounds the Rootback guardian. Use Enzo to bloom the graft beside the guardian’s braced shell, then use Dora to cut the exposed tie and open its belly. Watch its front claws lift before the shove. An upper shelf lets players go around the guardian while it turns, and a lower refuge is wide enough to recover on. The exit seal opens when it calms.

**Lantern Weir (4,19).** Set up a high graft from a low approach before dropping into the weir. A floor route passes two separated thorn strips with a broad landing between them. The upper route uses the branch and a ladder to avoid the thorns. Both routes return to the dry stone steps at the east end and a candle before the last fight. Players see the complete choice before committing.

**Mother of Grafts (6,19).** The last guardian stands in a sunken root bed with terraces on either side. Enzo’s light opens a gold bud on the side that has just attacked. Dora cuts that side’s tie to expose the core. Once the guard closes, the next attack marks the other side. The high ledges offer a way to switch sides while the floor roots are active. After the final bloom the orchard lamps stay lit and Killa’s grafts feed the road again. Killa joins the ending scene and points at the root lift, which has woken on the high east terrace; at 100% of the orchard the epilogue is harvest night (see [the expansions' endings](bellspire.md#the-expansions-endings-and-epilogues)).

## Art and acceptance

Paint each sheet separately for the pixel pass. Use the real Foundry camera size and hero proportions. Each outfit has a complete action set, including separate climb, landing, push, interact, Bloom and Shear poses where applicable. Plan four foes: a wall moth, a thorn hare, a clay beetle and a root mite. Give all guardian tells a readable shape and a light change. Keep empty background layers subordinate to ledge edges.

Before calling the expansion playable, photograph each room at two positions in the production build beside the Casting Halls. Sweep take-off points at 4 px intervals with both heroes and require at least 16 px of passing take-off positions for every intended jump. Run the keyboard route at desktop and both phone shapes. The planner uses the same solid and one-way geometry as the engine, with grafts included only in the states the route can actually unlock.
